# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions. Critique the
work, explain your reasoning, and allow contributors to correct mistakes.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and restrict participation.
For a conduct concern, contact the repository owner through an available contact
method on [their public profile](https://github.com/mithudso). Do not post private
incident details in an issue. This repository does not publish a dedicated
confidential reporting channel or response-time commitment.
